Installation Complete

Choroboros Beta is installed. Quit every audio app (DAWs, Pro Tools) before opening them again so they can load the new plug-ins.

Quick start

Pro Tools: Relaunch Pro Tools; AAX plug-ins are scanned at launch. Look for Choroboros Beta under your plug-in menus (e.g. by manufacturer).
Logic / GarageBand: Choroboros Beta appears as an Audio Unit (Kaizen DSP). The installer cleared the AU cache for faster discovery.
VST3 hosts: Rescan plug-ins in your DAW preferences if Choroboros Beta does not appear immediately.

AAX note: This AAX build uses PACE signing-only configuration — you do not need an iLok license to run it in Pro Tools.

Plug-in not showing?

Use your DAW’s rescan or restart. For Pro Tools, a full quit and reopen is usually enough. If an older Choroboros AAX was on the system, stale cache files may have been cleared during install.
