Choroboros Beta

Beta by Kaizen DSP

This installer places Choroboros Beta on your Mac. Choose which formats you need:

VST3Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, REAPER, FL Studio, Bitwig, and other VST3 hosts
Audio UnitLogic Pro, GarageBand, and other AU hosts
AAXAvid Pro Tools (system plug-in folder)
StandaloneUse Choroboros Beta without a DAW

Requires macOS 10.13 or later. An administrator password is required because plug-ins install to system locations shared by all users.
